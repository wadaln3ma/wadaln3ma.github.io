About

I work between the total station and the code editor, and I don't treat them as two separate jobs.

I'm a registered surveying engineer with the Saudi Council of Engineers and a certified Project Management Professional (PMP), based in Riyadh. My field work covers topographic, cadastral and engineering surveys with GNSS/RTK, total stations and UAVs.

On the software side I build web GIS applications, spatial databases and remote sensing workflows, and I'm currently completing Microsoft's .NET full-stack developer certificate to bring C# and ASP.NET Core into that toolkit.
